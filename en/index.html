08Stop 08 · FAQ

Questions before departure.

?How long does onboarding take?

Typically 8–10 weeks from contract to go-live. Delays usually come from waiting for legal texts, payment verification and DNS, which is why the inquiry already asks what you have.

?Where is our data?

In your own instance, on servers in the EU. The database is not shared with other operators, backups run daily, and at the end of the contract you receive an export of the database.

?Will our customers see our brand?

Yes: your logo, colors, domain, email sender, invoice letterhead and legal texts. BusFlow stays in the background.

?Does BusFlow work outside Germany?

Today it is tailored to the German market: euro, the German invoice format (Factur-X) and UStVA. Another country, currency or language means additional development, which we estimate at the meeting.

?How do customers pay?

By card via myPOS or with PayPal. You open the merchant account, and we connect it and test it with a trial payment.

?Who enters the fleet and price list?

We prepare templates and enter the data together with you in the “Fleet and price list” phase. There is currently no automatic import from an old system.

?Do drivers need a separate app?

No. The driver gets a Google Maps link with the whole route, and the trip sheet arrives by email before departure.

?What does it cost?

A one-off onboarding fee and a monthly fee for hosting, backup, monitoring, updates and support. We send the offer after the first meeting, based on the size of the fleet and the scope.
